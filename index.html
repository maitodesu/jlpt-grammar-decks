JLPT Grammar Decks

Every grammar point of the JLPT N5–N3 syllabus, as slides you can present. Each chapter is one self-contained page — arrow keys to move, ? for the presenter controls.

Every grammar point walks the same short path, so a point runs about 19 slides and a chapter is one sitting. Nothing here is read front to back — jump to the point you need.
  • Formation — what it attaches to, and how
  • Examples — the pattern in real sentences
  • Nuance & register — when it fits, and who says it
  • Contrast — how it differs from its lookalikes
  • Common mistakes — the errors worth pre-empting
  • Practice — questions, with answers you reveal
