お○○になる

Meaning · the general-purpose honorific frame - describes someone else's action while raising them

Japanese politeness runs on three separate systems. 丁寧ていねい語ご - です／ます - makes your speech polite to whoever is listening. 尊敬そんけい語ご raises the person you are talking about. 謙譲けんじょう語ご lowers you. お○○になる is the workhorse of the second group: take almost any verb, and it becomes a respectful description of what your customer, your teacher or your boss did.

お○○になる · Formation

Take the ます-stem, put お in front and になる behind. になる then conjugates as an ordinary う-verb (五段ごだん, Group 1).

お○○になる · Formation (cont.)

Attaches to Form Example
Verb (う-verb) お + ます-stem + になる 書かきます → お書かきになる
Verb (る-verb) お + ます-stem + になる 調しらべます → お調しらべになる
する-verb, Chinese-derived root ご + noun + になる 利用りようする → ご利用りようになる
する-verb, native root お + noun + になる 問とい合あわせする → お問とい合あわせになる
Polite / past お + stem + になります／になりました お使つかいになりました
Verb with its own honorific frame not used 食たべる → 召めし上あがる
One-mora ます-stem frame not used 見みる → ご覧らんになる、寝ねる → お休やすみになる

お○○になる · Formation (cont.)

Rule of thumb - if the ます-stem is only one mora long （見み、着き、寝ね、来き、し）, the frame will not carry it. Those verbs all have a dedicated honorific word instead: ご覧らんになる、お召めしになる、お休やすみになる、いらっしゃる、なさる.

お○○になる · Examples

先生せんせいは何時なんじにお帰かえりになりますか。
What time will you be leaving, sensei?

この本ほんをもうお読よみになりましたか。
Have you read this book already?

部長ぶちょうは昼ひるすぎにお出でかけになりました。
The department head went out in the early afternoon.

お客きゃく様さまがお選えらびになったのは、こちらの色いろでよろしいでしょうか。
Is this the colour you chose, madam?

お○○になる · Examples (cont.)

課長かちょうがお決きめになった方法ほうほうで進すすめます。
We'll go ahead with the method the section chief settled on.

エレベーターをご利用りようになる方かたは、右みぎの通路つうろへお回まわりください。
Those using the lift, please go round to the right-hand corridor.

皆みな様さまがお集あつまりになる時間じかんは午後ごご二時にじでよろしいでしょうか。
Would two o'clock suit as the time for everyone to gather?

新あたらしい計画けいかくについて、社長しゃちょうはどうお考かんがえになっているのでしょうか。
I wonder what the president makes of the new plan.

お○○になる · Nuance & register

This is business and service language. In a shop, a hospital, a hotel, a 会議かいぎ室しつ or a classroom it is completely ordinary; among friends it is not. Saying お食たべになりますか to a classmate is not "extra polite", it is strange - it puts a counter between the two of you. Overusing 敬語けいご socially is a real mistake, not a safe default.

Three things follow from the fact that お○○になる is 尊敬そんけい語ご:

  • Never about yourself. ❌私わたしがお作つくりになりました is the
    single commonest 敬語けいご error learners make. You cannot raise your own head. Your own actions take 謙譲けんじょう語ご - お作つくりしました

  • or plain ます.

お○○になる · Nuance & register (cont.)

  • Never doubled. ❌お読よみになられる stacks the frame on top of the
    られる honorific. That is 二重にじゅう敬語けいご, and it sounds overcooked rather than extra respectful. One layer is enough.
  • Not for your own side. Speaking to a customer about a colleague, even your
    own boss, you drop the honorific: 田中たなかはただいま席せきをはずしております - never お席せきをはずしになっています.

On the politeness ladder, お○○になる sits above れる／られる and below the set honorific verbs. Treat that as a tendency rather than a measurement: in most offices お書かきになる and 書かかれる are simply two acceptable choices, and which one a person reaches for depends on the workplace and on how far above them the other person stands.

お○○になる · お○○になる vs ～れる／～られる

Rule of thumb - both are honorific. られる is the light, everyday office form; お○○になる is heavier, more deferential, and unambiguous.

社長しゃちょうはもう**帰かえられました**。
The president has already left for the day.

社長しゃちょうはもうお帰かえりになりました。
The president has already left for the day.

お○○になる · お○○になる vs ～れる／～られる (cont.)

The second is the more respectful of the two, and it is what you would say to an outside visitor; the first is what one colleague says to another. The practical advantage of お○○になる is that it cannot be misread. 帰かえられる is also the passive and the potential, so 先生せんせいに来こられました can be read two ways, while 先生せんせいがお越こしになりました cannot.

お○○になる · お○○になる vs お○○する

Rule of thumb - same stem, opposite direction. になる raises the other person; する lowers you.

先生せんせいが荷物にもつをお持もちになります。
The teacher is going to carry the bags himself.

先生せんせいの荷物にもつをお持もちします。
I'll carry your bags for you, sensei.

One says the teacher does the carrying; the other offers to do it for him. Swapping them reverses who is being honoured, which is why the pair is worth drilling until it is automatic.

お○○になる · お○○になる vs the set honorific verbs

Rule of thumb - if a verb owns a dedicated honorific word, the frame is closed to it. Learn that short list; use the frame for everything else.

先生せんせいはもうお昼ひるを**召めし上あがりました**か。
Has sensei had lunch yet?

資料しりょうはもうご覧らんになりましたか。
Have you had a look at the documents yet?

お○○になる · お○○になる vs the set honorific verbs (cont.)

食たべる, 見みる, 言いう, する, 行いく, 来くる, いる and 寝ねる all belong to that closed set. Note that ご覧らんになる ends in になる but is not built from a ます-stem - ご覧らん is a noun, and the word has to be memorised whole.

お○○になる · Common mistakes

  • ❌ 私わたしが資料しりょうをお作つくりになりました。 → ✅ 私わたしが資料しりょうをお作つくりしました。
    Honorific language points away from the speaker. Describing your own work with お○○になる is the error a Japanese listener notices fastest.

  • ❌ 地図ちずをお見みになりましたか。 → ✅ 地図ちずをご覧らんになりましたか。
    見みる has a one-mora stem and its own honorific verb. The frame does not build on it.

  • ❌ 先生せんせいがお話はなしになられました。 → ✅ 先生せんせいがお話はなしになりました。
    Two honorific layers on one verb is 二重にじゅう敬語けいご. Pick one.

お○○になる · Common mistakes (cont.)

  • ❌ 課長かちょうがお説明せつめいになりました。 → ✅ 課長かちょうがご説明せつめいになりました。
    A Chinese-derived する-verb root takes ご: ご利用りよう、 ご出発しゅっぱつ、ご記入きにゅう.

  • ❌ ねえ、この漫画まんがお読よみになった? → ✅ ねえ、この漫画まんが読よんだ?
    Grammatical, wrong for the situation. Full 敬語けいご aimed at a friend reads as sarcasm or as sudden distance.

お○○になる · Practice 1

1. 先生せんせいは毎朝まいあさ新聞しんぶんを____読よ____になります。

先生せんせいは毎朝まいあさ新聞しんぶんをお読よみになります。

The frame is お + ます-stem + になる. The stem of 読よみます is 読よみ, so お goes in front of it and になります behind.

お○○になる · Practice 2

2. Choose: 係かかりの者ものが（ご案内あんないになります・ご案内あんないします）。

係かかりの者ものがご案内あんないします。

係かかりの者もの is a member of your own side, so the sentence has to lower rather than raise. ご○○になる would honour your own staff in front of a customer, which is exactly backwards.

お○○になる · Practice 3

3. Translate into Japanese: "What time will the president be arriving?"

社長しゃちょうは何時なんじにお着つきになりますか。

着つきます gives the stem 着つき, hence お着つきになります. ご到着とうちゃくになりますか works too, since 到着とうちゃく is a Chinese-derived root and takes ご.

お○○になる · Practice 4

4. Find and fix the error: 明日あした、私わたしは十時じゅうじにお越こしになります。

✅ 明日あした、私わたしは十時じゅうじに参まいります。

Two faults in one clause. The verb describes you, so it needs 謙譲けんじょう語ご, not 尊敬そんけい語ご. And 来くる is outside the frame anyway: the honorific for someone else coming is お越こしになる or いらっしゃる, while the humble word for your own coming is 参まいる.

お○○になる · Practice 5

5. Explain the difference in nuance: (a) 田中たなか様さまはいつ出発しゅっぱつされますか。 (b) 田中たなか様さまはいつご出発しゅっぱつになりますか。

Both are honorific and both are correct. (a) uses the される form, which is light and quick - the register of a colleague, or of a routine business email.

(b) is the heavier frame, and it is what a hotel clerk or a receptionist would say to a guest. It sounds more careful and more deliberate. The gap is one of degree, not of grammar: choosing (a) with a customer would not offend anyone, it would only sound a little brisk.

お○○ください

Meaning · "please do ○○" - an honorific request, the form used to customers, guests and audiences

お○○ください asks the listener to do something while raising them. It is the 尊敬そんけい語ご counterpart of ～てください: same job, but the listener is honoured rather than simply addressed politely. Every announcement you hear on a Japanese train, in a lift and in a shop is built out of this frame.

お○○ください · Formation

ください is the imperative of くださる, the honorific of くれる. Put it after お + the ます-stem.

Attaches to Form Example
Verb (う-verb) お + ます-stem + ください 待まちます → お待まちください
Verb (る-verb) お + ます-stem + ください 調しらべます → お調しらべください
する-verb, Chinese-derived root ご + noun + ください 確認かくにんする → ご確認かくにんください
する-verb, native root お + noun + ください 申もうし込こみする → お申もうし込こみください
Set honorific verb fixed form 見みる → ご覧らんください
Extra-polite service form + ませ お待まちくださいませ
Negative ("please don't") frame not used ご遠慮えんりょください／～ないでください

お○○ください · Formation (cont.)

Note - one-mora stems are shut out here for the same reason as in お○○になる: ❌お見みください、❌お来きください do not exist. Use ご覧らんください and お越こしください.

お○○ください · Examples

こちらでお待まちください。
Please wait here.

どうぞおかけください。
Please have a seat.

この線せんの内側うちがわまでお下さがりください。
Please step back behind this line.

温あたたかいうちにお召めし上あがりください。
Please eat it while it's still warm.

お○○ください · Examples (cont.)

足元あしもとにご注意ちゅういください。
Please mind your step.

順番じゅんばんにご案内あんないしますので、こちらにお並ならびください。
We'll show you through in turn, so please queue here.

ご不明ふめいな点てんがございましたら、いつでもお問とい合あわせください。
If anything is unclear, please get in touch at any time.

会場かいじょうにお入はいりの際さいは、受付うけつけでこちらの用紙ようしをお出だしください。
When you enter the hall, please hand this form in at reception.

お○○ください · Nuance & register

This frame is service and announcement language, and it carries a faint flavour of instruction. A station guard, a shop assistant, a nurse calling a patient and a website's help page all use it. Between friends it is bizarre - you do not tell a classmate ❌ちょっとお待まちください unless you are joking.

The instructional flavour matters when you want a personal favour. ください is still an imperative underneath, so a bare お書かきください to your boss can land like a directive from below. For asking a superior for something, shift to a question form:

お○○ください · Nuance & register (cont.)

  • お書かきいただけますか - "could you write it?"
  • お書かきいただけませんでしょうか - the softest, and standard in email.
  • 書かいていただけると助たすかります - "it would help me if you would."

ませ on the end（お待まちくださいませ、いらっしゃいませ）belongs to shop and hotel speech specifically. It is warm and formal at once, and it is heard far more from staff than from anyone else.

お○○ください · Nuance & register (cont.)

Two words about お召めし上あがりください and お求もとめください. Strictly, 召めし上あがる is already honorific, so adding お makes a double layer; usage guides nonetheless list these as established by custom, and you will hear them constantly. Treat them as fixed phrases rather than as models for building new ones.

One more tendency worth knowing: many speakers avoid お座すわりください in favour of おかけください, because お座すわり on its own is what you say to a dog. Plenty of hospitals and offices say お座すわりください anyway, so it is a preference rather than a rule - but おかけください is never the wrong choice.

お○○ください · お○○ください vs ～てください

Rule of thumb - ～てください is polite; お○○ください is polite and honorific. The first addresses the listener courteously, the second raises them.

ここに名前なまえを**書かいてください**。
Please write your name here.

こちらにお名前なまえをお書かきください。
Please write your name here.

お○○ください · お○○ください vs ～てください (cont.)

The first is what a teacher says to a class, or a friend to a friend. The second is what a bank clerk says to a customer - notice how ここ becomes こちら and 名前なまえ takes its prefix. 敬語けいご rarely arrives alone; the whole sentence shifts register together.

お○○ください · お○○ください vs お○○いただけますか

Rule of thumb - ください tells; いただけますか asks. When the favour is for your benefit, ask.

明日あしたまでにご返事へんじください。
Please reply by tomorrow.

明日あしたまでにご返事へんじいただけますでしょうか。
Might I ask you to reply by tomorrow?

お○○ください · お○○ください vs お○○いただけますか (cont.)

Both are polite. The first states what needs to happen and suits a notice or a form; the second leaves the decision with the listener and suits a request to a client or a superior. いただく is 謙譲けんじょう語ご, so the second sentence lowers you at the same time as it raises them - which is why it feels so much softer.

お○○ください · お○○ください vs ご遠慮ください

Rule of thumb - the frame has no negative. To ask someone not to do something, use a different verb: ご遠慮えんりょください or お控ひかえください.

車内しゃないでのお電話でんわはご遠慮えんりょください。
Please refrain from making phone calls on board.

写真しゃしんの撮影さつえいはお控ひかえください。
Please refrain from taking photographs.

お○○ください · お○○ください vs ご遠慮ください (cont.)

There is no ❌お話はなしにならないでください in ordinary use. Japanese public notices prefer to name a polite verb of refraining rather than to negate the request - it reads as an appeal to the listener's discretion instead of a ban.

お○○ください · Common mistakes

  • ❌ 明日あした会社かいしゃにお来きください。 → ✅ 明日あした会社かいしゃにお越こしください。
    来くる has no place in the frame. Use お越こしください, おいでください or いらしてください.

  • ❌ こちらのボタンをご確認かくにんしてください。 → ✅ こちらのボタンをご確認かくにんください。
    ご○○する is the humble frame, so ご確認かくにんしてください tells the listener to humble themselves. Drop the して.

  • ❌ メニューをお見みください。 → ✅ メニューをご覧らんください。
    One-mora stem, dedicated honorific verb. The same goes for お食たべください → お召めし上あがりください.

お○○ください · Common mistakes (cont.)

  • ❌ 部長ぶちょう、この書類しょるいにおサインください。 → ✅ 部長ぶちょう、この書類しょるいにご署名しょめいいただけますでしょうか。
    Two problems: loanwords do not take the prefix, and a direct ください to your own boss for your own paperwork is too flat. Ask with いただけますか.

お○○ください · Practice 1

1. 危あぶないですので、白線はくせんの内側うちがわまで____下さがり____。

危あぶないですので、白線はくせんの内側うちがわまでお下さがりください。

お + ます-stem + ください. 下さがります gives the stem 下さがり. This is the standard platform announcement, and it is honorific because it is addressed to passengers.

お○○ください · Practice 2

2. Choose: 資料しりょうを（ご覧らんください・拝見はいけんください）。

資料しりょうをご覧らんください。

拝見はいけんする is 謙譲けんじょう語ご - it lowers the person doing the looking. Telling the listener to 拝見はいけん orders them to be humble towards you, which is the opposite of what a request should do.

お○○ください · Practice 3

3. Translate into Japanese: "Please use the lift on the left."

左ひだりのエレベーターをご利用りようください。

利用りよう is a Chinese-derived する-verb root, so it takes ご and the する disappears entirely: ご利用りようください, not ❌ご利用りようしてください.

お○○ください · Practice 4

4. Find and fix the error: 館内かんないではおタバコをお吸すいにならないでください。

✅ 館内かんないでの喫煙きつえんはご遠慮えんりょください。

Two faults. Loanwords do not take the polite prefix, and the honorific frame has no natural negative - Japanese notices name a verb of refraining instead. 館内かんないではタバコをお吸すいにならないでください is understandable but sounds clumsy next to the fixed phrase.

お○○ください · Practice 5

5. Explain the difference in nuance: (a) 少すこ々お待まちください。 (b) 少すこ々お待まちいただけますか。

(a) is the standard service line - a receptionist, a phone operator, a waiter. It is honorific and perfectly courteous, but it tells the listener what will happen, which is fine when the waiting is part of the process.

(b) turns the same content into a question, so the listener is free to say no. It is what you use when the delay is your fault, or when you are asking a favour of someone senior. Between colleagues (a) would sound oddly official; from a shop assistant (b) can sound over-apologetic. Match the form to who owns the delay.

いらっしゃる

Meaning · the honorific verb covering いる, 行いく and 来くる all at once

いらっしゃる is 尊敬そんけい語ご: it raises the person it describes, so it is used about your teacher, your client, your guest - never about yourself. Three ordinary verbs collapse into it, and Japanese speakers do not find that ambiguous, because the situation always says which one is meant.

いらっしゃる · Formation

Plain verb Honorific Example
いる いらっしゃる 先生せんせいは部屋へやにいらっしゃいます
行いく いらっしゃる どちらへいらっしゃいますか
来くる いらっしゃる よくいらっしゃいました
～ている ～ていらっしゃる 待まっていらっしゃいます
Noun + です (about a person) ～でいらっしゃる 社長しゃちょうでいらっしゃいます

It is a う-verb, but its ます-form is irregular - the り you expect becomes い. Four verbs share this quirk, and they are all 敬語けいご:

おっしゃる おっしゃいます ❌おっしゃります
なさる なさいます ❌なさります
くださる くださいます ❌くださります

いらっしゃる · Examples

先生せんせいは教室きょうしつにいらっしゃいますか。
Is the teacher in the classroom?

どちらへいらっしゃいますか。
Where are you off to?

遠とおいところ、よくいらっしゃいました。
Thank you for coming all this way.

明日あしたの会かいには何名なんめいほどいらっしゃいますか。
Roughly how many people will be coming to tomorrow's gathering?

いらっしゃる · Examples (cont.)

部長ぶちょうは今いま、会議室かいぎしつにはいらっしゃいません。
The department head isn't in the meeting room at the moment.

山田やまだ先生せんせいは来週らいしゅう大阪おおさかからいらっしゃるそうです。
I hear Professor Yamada is coming up from Osaka next week.

お客きゃく様さまが先さきほどから受付うけつけで**待まっていらっしゃいます**。
The customer has been waiting at reception for a little while now.

失礼しつれいですが、田中たなか様さまでいらっしゃいますか。
Excuse me - would you be Mr Tanaka?

いらっしゃる · Nuance & register

いらっしゃる is the first 敬語けいご verb most learners can actually use, because it turns up everywhere: shop doorways, phone calls, introductions, reception desks. It belongs to business, service and formal social speech. Saying it about a friend - ❌ゆうべ友ともだちが家いえにいらっしゃった - is not extra-polite, it is odd, because you are honouring someone who is on your own level.

Some texture:

いらっしゃる · Nuance & register (cont.)

  • いらっしゃいませ is not an invitation to come. It is a shop and restaurant
    greeting, roughly "welcome", and it expects no reply at all.
  • いらっしゃい on its own is warm and informal - a host to a guest, an adult to
    a child, a market stall to passers-by.
  • いらして／いらしてください is the contracted て-form. It is softer and a
    little lighter than いらっしゃって, and it leans towards polite conversational speech rather than announcements.
  • お越こしになる, おいでになる, お見みえになる are alternatives for
    the "come" sense, each a shade more formal than plain いらっしゃる.

いらっしゃる · Nuance & register (cont.)

The trap that catches every English speaker is the in-group rule. Japanese politeness is not about rank in the abstract; it is about which side of a line the person stands on. Inside your company, 部長ぶちょう outranks you and takes 尊敬そんけい語ご. The moment you speak to somebody outside the company - a client, a supplier, a caller - your whole company becomes your in-group, and your boss is lowered along with you:

  • To a colleague: 部長ぶちょうはもうお帰かえりになりました。
  • To a client: 田中たなかは本日ほんじつ外出がいしゅつしております。

いらっしゃる · Nuance & register (cont.)

No title, no さん, and おります rather than いらっしゃいます. It feels disloyal to an English speaker; in Japanese it is exactly the loyal thing to do, because lowering your own side is how you raise the customer.

いらっしゃる · いらっしゃる vs おる

Rule of thumb - いらっしゃる for people you are raising; おる for yourself and for anyone on your own side of the line.

社長しゃちょうは三階さんがいにいらっしゃいます。
The president is on the third floor.

私わたしどもは明日あしたも店みせにおります。
We'll be at the shop tomorrow as well.

いらっしゃる · いらっしゃる vs おる (cont.)

The same phone call can need both, one clause apart. An outside caller asking 田中たなかさんはいらっしゃいますか is right - from where they stand, 田中たなか is an outsider and deserves the honorific. Your reply, 田中たなかはただいま席せきをはずしております, is also right, because from where you stand he is family.

いらっしゃる · いらっしゃる vs 参る・伺う

Rule of thumb - いらっしゃる is them going or coming; 参まいる and 伺うかがう are you going or coming.

先生せんせいは三時さんじにいらっしゃいます。
Sensei will be arriving at three.

三時さんじに研究室けんきゅうしつへ**伺うかがいます**。
I'll come to your office at three.

伺うかがう is used when the place you are going belongs to somebody you respect; 参まいる is the plainer humble word and works even when nobody in particular is being honoured - 電車でんしゃが参まいります on a platform announcement is about a train, not a person.

いらっしゃる · いらっしゃる vs おいでになる・お見えになる

Rule of thumb - all three are honorific. いらっしゃる is the default; おいでになる is more formal; お見みえになる only ever means "come".

会長かいちょうが会場かいじょうにおいでになりました。
The chairman has arrived at the venue.

お約束やくそくの方かたがお見みえになりました。
Your appointment has arrived.

いらっしゃる · いらっしゃる vs おいでになる・お見えになる (cont.)

Because いらっしゃる covers three meanings, the alternatives are sometimes chosen simply for clarity: お見みえになりました can only be an arrival, so nobody has to work out whether the person came, went or is present.

いらっしゃる · Common mistakes

  • ❌ 先生せんせいは明日あしたいらっしゃります。 → ✅ 先生せんせいは明日あしたいらっしゃいます。
    The り becomes い in the ます-form. The same applies to おっしゃいます, なさいます and くださいます.

  • ❌ 明日あした、私わたしが会社かいしゃにいらっしゃいます。 → ✅ 明日あした、私わたしが会社かいしゃに**参まいります**。
    尊敬そんけい語ご about yourself is the classic error. You cannot raise your own head - use the humble verb.

  • ❌ （to a client）弊社へいしゃの社長しゃちょうは今いまいらっしゃいません。 → ✅ 社長しゃちょうの佐藤さとうは本日ほんじつおりません。
    The in-group rule. Talking to an outsider, your own president is lowered like everyone else on your side - and loses the さん.

いらっしゃる · Common mistakes (cont.)

  • ❌ こちらの資料しりょうはいらっしゃいますか。 → ✅ こちらの資料しりょうはございますか。
    いらっしゃる honours a person. Objects take ある and its polite form ございます; there is nobody there to raise.

いらっしゃる · Practice 5

5. Explain the difference in nuance: (a) いらっしゃいませ。 (b) いらっしゃい。

(a) is service language. A shop assistant, a waiter or a hotel receptionist says it as you walk in, and it is not really addressed to you as an individual - no reply is expected.

(b) is the same word without the ませ, and dropping ませ drops the formality. It is what a host says opening the door to a guest, or a parent to a child's friend: friendly and personal. A market trader shouting いらっしゃい, いらっしゃい is using it as a call to passers-by, which is friendlier still.

なさる

Meaning · the honorific of する - "to do", said about someone you are raising

なさる is 尊敬そんけい語ご. Because する combines with hundreds of nouns, なさる gives you an honorific for every one of them at a stroke: 勉強べんきょうなさる、運転うんてんなさる、ご結婚けっこんなさる. It is also the verb behind the two most useful service questions in Japanese, どうなさいますか and いかがなさいましたか.

部長ぶちょうはこの件けんをどのように処理しょりなさるおつもりでしょうか。
How does the department head intend to handle this matter?

なさる · Nuance & register

なさる is squarely business, service and formal-social language. Shop staff, receptionists, doctors, flight attendants and hotel clerks use it constantly, mostly in the fixed openers どうなさいますか（"what would you like to do?"）, いかがなさいますか（the more formal version）and どうなさいましたか（"what's wrong?"）. To a friend, ❌何なにをなさっているんですか would be comic.

Because it is 尊敬そんけい語ご it obeys the same two hard limits as the rest of the system. It never describes your own action - that is いたす - and it never describes your own side when you are speaking to an outsider. On the phone to a customer, ❌担当たんとうの者ものが確認かくにんなさいます honours your own colleague; the sentence you want is 担当たんとうの者ものが確認かくにんいたします.

なさる · Nuance & register (cont.)

される is the lighter alternative: 部長ぶちょうはどうされますか is perfectly polite and rather more everyday than 部長ぶちょうはどうなさいますか. As a tendency, なさる is felt as warmer and more attentive, and it is heard more often from service staff and in careful formal speech; される is the workhorse of ordinary office Japanese. Neither is wrong in the other's setting.

Do not stack layers. ❌おなさりになる and ❌なさられる are 二重にじゅう敬語けいご; なさる on its own is already the honorific.

なさる · なさる vs いたす

Rule of thumb - なさる for what they do; いたす for what you do. Same verb, opposite directions.

お客きゃく様さまはどちらになさいますか。
Which will you have, madam?

私わたしどもがご用意よういいたします。
We'll get it ready for you.

This pair is the cleanest illustration of the whole three-way system. Both sentences are polite to the customer; the first gets there by lifting her, the second by lowering the speaker. Using なさる about yourself does not merely sound odd - it claims a status you have just given to somebody else.

なさる · なさる vs される

Rule of thumb - both are honorific. される is lighter and unmarked; なさる is more attentive and more distinctly 敬語けいご.

課長かちょうはいつ出張しゅっちょうされますか。
When are you going on the business trip, 課長かちょう?

会長かいちょうはいつ出張しゅっちょうなさいますか。
When will the chairman be going on his trip?

なさる · なさる vs される (cont.)

There is one practical reason to reach for なさる: される is also the passive. 注意ちゅういされました can mean "you kindly took care" or "I got told off", and 注意ちゅういなさいました can only mean the first.

なさる · なさる vs ～なさい

Rule of thumb - なさる looks up; ～なさい looks down. They come from the same root and point in opposite social directions.

宿題しゅくだいを先さきにしなさい。
Do your homework first.

宿題しゅくだいはもうなさいましたか。
Have you done your homework yet? (said respectfully)

なさる · なさる vs ～なさい (cont.)

～なさい is softer than the bare imperative しろ, which is why parents and teachers like it - but softness is not respect. Aimed upward it is an insult, so ❌先生せんせい、こちらに書かきなさい must be 先生せんせい、こちらにお書かきください.

なさる · Common mistakes

  • ❌ 社長しゃちょうは何時なんじに出発しゅっぱつなさりますか。 → ✅ 社長しゃちょうは何時なんじに出発しゅっぱつなさいますか。
    The り turns into い before ます. Same irregularity as いらっしゃいます and くださいます.

  • ❌ 私わたしがご説明せつめいなさいます。 → ✅ 私わたしがご説明せつめいいたします。
    尊敬そんけい語ご pointed at yourself. Your own actions take 謙譲けんじょう語ご.

  • ❌ お客きゃく様さま、どうしましたか。 → ✅ お客きゃく様さま、どうなさいましたか。
    Grammatically fine, socially thin. しましたか is only 丁寧ていねい語ご - polite speech with no honorific in it - and a customer expects to be raised, not merely addressed politely.

なさる · Common mistakes (cont.)

  • ❌ 先生せんせいが説明せつめいなさられました。 → ✅ 先生せんせいが説明せつめいなさいました。
    なさる plus られる is two honorifics on one verb. One is the ceiling.

何なににする - "go for which one" - becomes 何なにになさる when the chooser is the person you are serving. This is the standard restaurant line.

なさる · Practice 4

～なさい is a command directed downward - fine from a teacher to a pupil, unacceptable to your own department head. A request upward needs the honorific frame, and softening it into a question softens it further.

なさる · Practice 5

5. Explain the difference in nuance: (a) 先生せんせいは何なにを研究けんきゅうしていますか。 (b) 先生せんせいは何なにを研究けんきゅうなさっていますか。

Both are polite in form, and neither is rude. (a) uses only 丁寧ていねい語ご: the ます makes your speech courteous, but the verb itself says nothing about the teacher's standing. It is what a student might say to a lecturer they know well.

(b) adds 尊敬そんけい語ご on top, so the teacher is lifted by the verb as well as addressed politely. It suits a first meeting, an interview or a formal setting. The difference is not politeness versus rudeness - it is distance and deference.

敬語の動詞 · Nuance & register

Hold the three-way system in mind and none of this is guesswork. 丁寧ていねい語ご（です・ます）makes your speech polite to the listener. 尊敬そんけい語ご raises the subject of the verb. 謙譲けんじょう語ご lowers the subject of the verb, which raises whoever the action is aimed at. Choosing a form is therefore a question about who is doing the verb, not about how polite you feel.

Two refinements worth having early:

敬語の動詞 · Nuance & register (cont.)

The humble column has two halves. 伺うかがう, 申もうし上あげる, 拝見はいけんする and お目めにかかる need a specific person on the receiving end who deserves the respect. 参まいる, 申もうす and おる do not - they simply lower and formalise, which is why 電車でんしゃが参まいります works on a platform announcement even though a train respects nobody. That second group is sometimes labelled 丁重ていちょう語ご, and it is the group you use for your own side in front of an outsider.

敬語の動詞 · Nuance & register (cont.)

In-group before rank. English speakers expect politeness to track seniority. Japanese tracks the boundary of your group first. Talking to a client, your own 部長ぶちょう is lowered exactly like you: not ❌部長ぶちょうがおっしゃっていました but 部長ぶちょうの中村なかむらが申もうしておりました - no さん, humble verb, and the title placed before the surname rather than after it. Inside the office, with only colleagues listening, おっしゃいました is right again. Nothing about the boss changed; the audience did.

Some smaller points:

敬語の動詞 · Nuance & register (cont.)

  • いただく does double duty - humble "eat/drink" and humble "receive". The
    context separates them.
  • 存ぞんじ上あげる is used about people
    （お名前なまえは存ぞんじ上あげております）, and 存ぞんじる about facts.
  • 承うけたまわる is heavy formal business language - order-taking, phone
    messages - and is rare in speech otherwise.
  • お伺うかがいします is strictly a double layer, but it is so established that
    usage guides let it stand. Do not extend the pattern to new verbs.

And the honest register note: this is the language of work and of service. It is not a politer version of everyday Japanese that you can switch on at will. Using it with friends puts a formal wall between you, and Japanese speakers read that wall as coldness or as mockery.

敬語の動詞 · 召し上がる vs いただく

Rule of thumb - 召めし上あがる is them eating; いただく is you eating. Both mean "eat", and they are never interchangeable.

冷さめないうちに**召めし上あがって**ください。
Do please eat before it gets cold.

おいしいお菓子かしをいただきました。
I had some lovely cakes.

敬語の動詞 · 召し上がる vs いただく (cont.)

The commonest single 敬語けいご error learners make is turning the honorific on themselves, and this is where it happens most: ❌私わたしはもう召めし上あがりました. You have just told the room you consider yourself the honoured guest. The word for your own eating is いただきました - the same word as in the いただきます said before a meal.

敬語の動詞 · おっしゃる vs 申す・申し上げる

Rule of thumb - おっしゃる is their speaking; 申もうす is yours. 申もうし上あげる adds a person you are speaking to and deserves.

先生せんせいは何なにかおっしゃいましたか。
Did sensei say anything?

はじめまして、鈴木すずきと**申もうします**。
How do you do - my name is Suzuki.

Introducing yourself with ❌鈴木すずきとおっしゃいます would mean "I am called Suzuki, the honourable gentleman". The reverse error is just as common: asking a customer ❌何なんと申もうしますか lowers the customer.

敬語の動詞 · 伺う vs 参る

Rule of thumb - both are humble "go/come". 伺うかがう requires a person at the destination whom you respect; 参まいる does not.

来週らいしゅう、先生せんせいのお宅たくへ**伺うかがいます**。
I'll visit your home next week, sensei.

三番さんばん線せんに電車でんしゃが**参まいります**。
A train is arriving at platform three.

敬語の動詞 · 伺う vs 参る (cont.)

Announcements, weather reports and formal self-description use 参まいる freely; you cannot substitute 伺うかがう into any of them, because there is nobody to honour. Note too that 伺うかがう has a second job - humble "ask" - so ちょっと伺うかがいますが is "may I ask you something?"

敬語の動詞 · Common mistakes

  • ❌ 私わたしは会議かいぎの資料しりょうをご覧らんになりました。 → ✅ 私わたしは会議かいぎの資料しりょうを**拝見はいけんしました**。
    尊敬そんけい語ご about yourself. Looking at somebody's documents is your own action, so it takes the humble word.

  • ❌ （to a customer）社長しゃちょうさんがそうおっしゃいました。 → ✅ 社長しゃちょうの佐藤さとうがそう申もうしておりました。
    The in-group rule. Your own president is lowered in front of an outsider, referred to by bare surname, with the title in front.

  • ❌ 先生せんせいのお話はなしをお聞ききになりました。 → ✅ 先生せんせいのお話はなしを**伺うかがいました**。
    Same error with a different verb. When you are the listener, the humble verb is 伺うかがう.

敬語の動詞 · Common mistakes (cont.)

  • ❌ その方かたのお名前なまえは**存ぞんじております**。 → ✅ その方かたのお名前なまえは**存ぞんじ上あげております**。
    Knowing a person takes 存ぞんじ上あげる; 存ぞんじる on its own is for facts and things.

  • ❌ ねえ、もう**召めし上あがった**? → ✅ ねえ、もう食たべた?
    Correct grammar, wrong world. Full 敬語けいご between friends reads as a joke at the listener's expense.

敬語の動詞 · Practice 1

1. どうぞ、温あたたかいうちに____ください。

どうぞ、温あたたかいうちに**召めし上あがって**ください。

The person eating is your guest, so the verb must raise them. There is no such form as ❌お食たべください, and お召めし上あがりください is the fixed extra-polite variant of the same thing.

敬語の動詞 · Practice 2

2. Choose: 明日あした、部長ぶちょうのお宅たくへ（いらっしゃいます・伺うかがいます）。

明日あした、部長ぶちょうのお宅たくへ**伺うかがいます**。

The speaker is the one travelling, so the verb lowers the speaker. 伺うかがう rather than 参まいる because the destination belongs to a person being honoured.

敬語の動詞 · Practice 3

3. Translate into Japanese, as a shop assistant to a customer: "Have you seen this month's catalogue?"

今月こんげつのカタログはもうご覧らんになりましたか。

The customer is doing the looking, so 見みる becomes the honorific ご覧らんになる. 拝見はいけんなさいましたか is wrong twice over - a humble verb dressed in an honorific ending.

敬語の動詞 · Practice 4

4. Find and fix the error: 山田やまだ部長ぶちょうは今いま席せきにいらっしゃいませんが、戻もどりましたらご連絡れんらくなさいます。（said to a client on the phone）

✅ 部長ぶちょうの山田やまだは今いま席せきを外はずしておりますが、戻もどりましたらご連絡れんらくいたします。

いらっしゃる and なさる both honour your own department head in front of a customer. Lower him instead: humble おる and いたす, bare surname, and the title moved in front of the name.

敬語の動詞 · Practice 5

5. Explain the difference in nuance: (a) 明日あした大阪おおさかへ参まいります。 (b) 明日あした大阪おおさかへ伺うかがいます。

Both lower the speaker, so both are humble. (a) simply makes the statement formal: it works whether you are visiting a client, going to a conference or moving house, because 参まいる does not point at anybody.

(b) says there is a particular person in Osaka whom you are going to see and whom you respect - a client, a teacher, a customer. Said about a purely private trip it sounds off, because the respect in the verb has nowhere to land.

お○○する

Meaning · the general-purpose humble frame - describes your own action while lowering you

お○○する is the mirror image of お○○になる. Same prefix, same ます-stem, but する on the end instead of になる, and the whole thing points the other way: it lowers the speaker, and by lowering the speaker it raises the person the action is done for. This is 謙譲けんじょう語ご, and it is the half of 敬語けいご that learners most often leave out.

お○○する · Formation

Attaches to Form Example
Verb (う-verb) お + ます-stem + する 持もちます → お持もちする
Verb (る-verb) お + ます-stem + する 届とどけます → お届とどけする
する-verb, Chinese-derived root ご + noun + する 説明せつめいする → ご説明せつめいする
する-verb, native root お + noun + する 手伝てつだいする → お手伝てつだいする
More humble お／ご + stem + いたす お持もちいたします
Most formal (written) お／ご + stem + 申もうし上あげる ご案内あんない申もうし上あげます
Ongoing / standing state お／ご + stem + しております お待まちしております
Verb with a set humble word frame not used 見みる → 拝見はいけんする

お○○する · Formation (cont.)

Rule of thumb - the frame needs a recipient: somebody the action is done to, for, or in front of, whom you respect. お持もちします works because you carry it for them. ❌お寝ねします and ❌お散歩さんぽします do not, because nobody is on the receiving end.

お○○する · Examples

お荷物にもつをお持もちします。
Let me take your bag.

この傘かさをお貸かしします。
I'll lend you this umbrella.

使つかい方かたをご説明せつめいします。
I'll explain how it works.

ロビーでお待まちしております。
I'll be waiting in the lobby.

お○○する · Examples (cont.)

書類しょるいは明日あしたまでにお届とどけします。
I'll get the documents to you by tomorrow.

少すこしお尋たずねしたいことがあるのですが、よろしいでしょうか。
There's something I'd like to ask you, if I may.

受付うけつけから会場かいじょうまで、係かかりの者ものがご案内あんないします。
A member of staff will show you from reception through to the hall.

新あたらしいサービスにつきましては、あらためて資料しりょうをお送おくりしますので、ご確認かくにんいただければ幸さいわいです。
I'll send you the material on the new service separately, so I'd be grateful if you would take a look.

お○○する · Nuance & register

Everything here belongs to work, service and formal dealings with strangers. In a shop, on a client call, in a business email it is the normal way to describe what you are about to do; between friends it is not, and お貸かししようか to a classmate sounds like you are role-playing a hotel clerk.

The ladder inside the frame is worth knowing:

  • お送おくりします - standard humble. Fine for almost everything.
  • お送おくりいたします - いたす is itself the humble する, so this is
    one notch lower and one notch more formal. Common in business speech.
  • お送おくり申もうし上あげます - ceremonial and written. Notices,
    formal letters, wedding invitations.

お○○する · Nuance & register (cont.)

Treat that as a gradient rather than three fixed rungs: most speakers move between the first two without thinking about it, and the third is rare enough that using it in conversation would draw attention.

しております is the humble ～ている, and it is everywhere in service Japanese because so much of that language describes standing states rather than single acts - お待まちしております, ご連絡れんらくをお待まちしております, お問とい合あわせをお受うけしております.

お○○する · Nuance & register (cont.)

Two limits to keep straight. First, the frame never describes somebody else's action: ❌先生せんせいがお話はなしします turns your teacher into your servant. Second, an action with no human on the other end will not take it, which is why "I'll go home early today" cannot be ❌早はやくお帰かえりします - say 失礼しつれいします or 早はやく帰かえらせていただきます instead.

お○○する · お○○する vs お○○になる

Rule of thumb - identical up to the last word. する = my action, lowered. になる = their action, raised.

先生せんせいに新あたらしい住所じゅうしょをお知しらせします。
I'll let sensei know my new address.

先生せんせいが新あたらしい住所じゅうしょをお知しらせになりました。
Sensei let us know his new address.

Get the pair backwards and you say the opposite of what you mean. It is worth practising them as a drill: pick a verb, build both, and say out loud who is doing which.

お○○する · お○○する vs ～させていただく

Rule of thumb - お○○する says "I'll do it for you"; ～させていただく says "I'll take the liberty, with your permission".

来週らいしゅうの予定よていをご連絡れんらくします。
I'll let you know next week's schedule.

本日ほんじつは早はやめに**閉しめさせていただきます**。
We'll be closing early today.

させていただく frames the action as something you are permitted to do, so it fits things that affect the listener without being for them - closing early, taking leave, declining. It has spread so far in recent business Japanese that it is regularly criticised as overused; where the action really is a service you are performing, お○○する is the cleaner choice.

お○○する · お○○する vs plain ます

Rule of thumb - ます is 丁寧ていねい語ご only: polite speech, no humility. お○○する adds the humility.

明日あした資料しりょうを**送おくります**。
I'll send the material tomorrow.

明日あした資料しりょうをお送おくりします。
I'll send the material to you tomorrow.

Both are polite; neither is rude. The first is what you write to a colleague, the second to a customer. This is the distinction to keep hold of when people say 敬語けいご is "polite Japanese": です／ます politeness and 謙譲けんじょう語ご humility are separate dials, and you can turn one without the other.

お○○する · Common mistakes

  • ❌ 部長ぶちょうが会議かいぎの内容ないようをご説明せつめいします。（to a colleague）→ ✅ 部長ぶちょうが会議かいぎの内容ないようをご説明せつめいになります。
    The frame lowers whoever is doing the verb. Applied to your department head in front of colleagues, it demotes him.

  • ❌ 私わたしがお説明せつめいします。 → ✅ 私わたしがご説明せつめいします。
    説明せつめい is a Chinese-derived root, so the prefix is ご. Compare ご連絡れんらくします, ご報告ほうこくします, ご案内あんないします.

  • ❌ 先生せんせいの論文ろんぶんをお見みしました。 → ✅ 先生せんせいの論文ろんぶんを**拝見はいけんしました**。
    One-mora stems are shut out of the frame, and 見みる has its own humble verb.

お○○する · Common mistakes (cont.)

  • ❌ 今日きょうは用事ようじがあるので、早はやくお帰かえりします。 → ✅ 今日きょうは用事ようじがあるので、お先さきに失礼しつれいします。
    Going home is not done for anybody, so there is no recipient for the humility to reach. Worse, お帰かえり is strongly associated with the honorific, so the sentence sounds as if you are honouring yourself.

お○○する · Practice 1

1. 重おもそうですね。私わたしが____持もち____。

重おもそうですね。私わたしがお持もちします。

お + ます-stem + する. The action is done for the listener, which is exactly the condition the frame needs. お持もちいたします is a shade more formal and equally correct.

お○○する · Practice 2

2. Choose: 係かかりの者ものが後あとほど（ご連絡れんらくします・ご連絡れんらくになります）。

係かかりの者ものが後あとほどご連絡れんらくします。

係かかりの者もの is on your own side, so the sentence lowers. ご連絡れんらくになります would honour your own colleague in front of the person you are speaking to.

お○○する · Practice 3

3. Translate into Japanese, as a hotel receptionist: "I'll show you to your room."

お部屋へやまでご案内あんないいたします。

案内あんない takes ご. いたします rather than します because hotel speech sits at the formal end of the range; ご案内あんないします is also correct and slightly lighter.

お○○する · Practice 4

4. Find and fix the error: 社長しゃちょうはこれから新あたらしい計画けいかくについてお話はなしします。

✅ 社長しゃちょうはこれから新あたらしい計画けいかくについてお話はなしになります。

The subject is the president, so the frame must raise rather than lower. Note that if you were saying this to an outside audience about your own company's president, you would drop the honorific again and say 社長しゃちょうの佐藤さとうがご説明せつめいいたします.

お○○する · Practice 5

5. Explain the difference in nuance: (a) 窓口まどぐちでお預あずかりします。 (b) 窓口まどぐちで預あずかります。

Both mean the counter will hold the item, and both are polite in form. (a) uses the humble frame, so the speaker is lowered and the customer raised; it is the sentence a bank clerk or a cloakroom attendant says, and the courtesy is aimed at a specific person standing in front of them.

(b) is only 丁寧ていねい語ご. It reports the arrangement neutrally, the way a colleague explains a procedure to another colleague, or a sign states a rule. Nothing in it honours the listener.

いたす

Meaning · the humble of する - "to do", said about your own action

いたす is 謙譲けんじょう語ご. It replaces する when the person doing the verb is you, or anyone on your side of the group boundary, and by lowering that person it raises whoever you are speaking to. It is the direct opposite number of なさる, and between them the two cover every する verb in the language.

いたす · Formation

Attaches to Form Example
する いたす する → いたす
Noun + する Noun + いたす 失礼しつれいする → 失礼しつれいいたす
Noun + する, with prefix ご／お + Noun + いたす 案内あんないする → ご案内あんないいたす
Verb (humble frame) お + ます-stem + いたす 届とどけます → お届とどけいたす
～ている いたしております 準備じゅんびしている → 準備じゅんびいたしております

Unlike なさる and いらっしゃる, いたす is a completely regular う-verb:

いたす · Formation (cont.)

Form Shape
Polite いたします
Past polite いたしました
Negative polite いたしません
Plain negative いたさない
て-form いたして／いたしまして

Note - in practice you will almost never meet the plain form in speech. いたす lives in the ます-form; a plain いたす turns up mainly in written notices and in fixed compounds.

いたす · Examples

その仕事しごとは私わたしがいたします。
I'll take care of that job.

失礼しつれいいたします。
Excuse me. / I'll be on my way.

どういたしまして。
Not at all - you're very welcome.

準備じゅんびは私わたしどもがいたしますので、ご安心あんしんください。
We'll handle the preparations, so please don't worry.

いたす · Examples (cont.)

ただいま担当たんとうの者ものが確認かくにんいたします。
Someone from the relevant team will check that right now.

承知しょうちいたしました。すぐに手配てはいいたします。
Understood. I'll arrange it straight away.

本日ほんじつは貴重きちょうなお時間じかんをいただき、感謝かんしゃいたします。
Thank you for giving us your valuable time today.

来週らいしゅうの打うち合あわせにつきましては、あらためてメールでご連絡れんらくいたします。
As for next week's meeting, I'll be in touch again by email.

いたす · Nuance & register

いたします is one of the most-used words in Japanese working life, largely because it is welded into a handful of phrases you will say every day:

  • よろしくお願ねがいいたします - closes almost every business email.
  • 失礼しつれいいたします - entering a room, leaving a room, ending a call.
  • 承知しょうちいたしました - "understood", to a customer or a superior.
  • どういたしまして - a frozen phrase built on this verb, used to wave away
    thanks.

いたす · Nuance & register (cont.)

There is a useful register ordering for acknowledging an instruction, though it is a tendency rather than a rule: かしこまりました is the most deferential and belongs to service staff, 承知しょうちいたしました is the business standard, わかりました is neutral, and 了解りょうかいしました is felt by many people to be too casual to send upward. Plenty of workplaces use 了解りょうかいです internally without anyone minding; to a client it is worth avoiding.

いたす · Nuance & register (cont.)

いたす also has a slightly different flavour depending on what it is attached to. In ご連絡れんらくいたします it is aimed at a specific person you are serving. In 失礼しつれいいたします or 開店かいてん時間じかんを変更へんこういたしました it simply makes the statement formal and self-effacing, with nobody in particular being honoured - the same job that 参まいる and 申もうす do. Both uses are humble; only the second works in an announcement.

Finally, the limit that defines the word: いたす never describes somebody else's action when you are honouring them. ❌先生せんせいが説明せつめいいたしました lowers your teacher. The exception is your own in-group in front of an outsider, where lowering is exactly right: 部長ぶちょうの中村なかむらがご説明せつめいいたします.

いたす · いたす vs なさる

Rule of thumb - いたす for what I do, なさる for what you or they do. One verb, two directions.

お席せきはどちらになさいますか。
Which table would you like?

それでは、三十分さんじゅっぷんほどでお持もちいたします。
In that case, I'll bring it to you in about thirty minutes.

One restaurant exchange, two verbs. Everything the customer does takes なさる; everything the staff do takes いたす. A single conversation swings between them constantly, and the swing follows the subject of each verb, not the overall tone of the conversation.

いたす · いたす vs する

Rule of thumb - します is polite speech. いたします is polite speech plus humility. They are not two levels of the same dial.

明日あしたの朝あさ、電話でんわします。
I'll phone tomorrow morning.

明日あしたの朝あさ、お電話でんわいたします。
I'll telephone you tomorrow morning.

The first is perfectly polite and entirely normal to a colleague or a friend's parent. The second lowers you in front of the listener and belongs to a customer, a client or a professor. Reaching for いたします with a friend does not make you sound respectful; it makes you sound like a call centre.

いたす · お○○いたす vs お○○する

Rule of thumb - both are humble. いたす is one notch more formal, because the humble frame and the humble verb are working together.

資料しりょうをお渡わたしします。
I'll hand you the material.

資料しりょうをお渡わたしいたします。
I'll hand you the material.

いたす · お○○いたす vs お○○する (cont.)

Strictly this doubles up, but it is long-established and nobody objects to it - unlike ❌お読よみになられる on the honorific side, which does draw comment. Use お○○します in ordinary business speech and お○○いたします when you are being formal: a first meeting, a customer, a public announcement.

いたす · Common mistakes

  • ❌ 先生せんせいが説明せつめいいたしました。 → ✅ 先生せんせいが説明せつめいなさいました。
    A humble verb applied to the person you mean to honour. It says your teacher demeaned himself in order to explain.

  • ❌ お客きゃく様さま、どういたしますか。 → ✅ お客きゃく様さま、どうなさいますか。
    The customer is doing the deciding, so the verb must raise. どういたしましょうか is fine, because there the speaker is offering to act.

  • ❌ 部長ぶちょう、会議かいぎに出席しゅっせきいたしますか。 → ✅ 部長ぶちょう、会議かいぎに出席しゅっせきなさいますか。
    Inside the company your department head is above you and takes 尊敬そんけい語ご. Only in front of an outsider does he get lowered.

いたす · Common mistakes (cont.)

  • ❌ （to a client）了解りょうかいしました。 → ✅ 承知しょうちいたしました。
    Not a grammar error, a register one. 了解りょうかい reads as flat and slightly casual coming from the serving side of a business relationship.

いたす · Practice 1

1. ご質問しつもんには私わたしどもが責任せきにんをもってお答こたえ____。

ご質問しつもんには私わたしどもが責任せきにんをもってお答こたえいたします。

The subject is 私わたしども, so the verb is humble. お + ます-stem + いたす is the more formal of the two humble frames, which suits a written undertaking like this one. お答こたえします would also be correct.

いたす · Practice 2

2. Choose: 社長しゃちょうは明日あした大阪おおさかへ（出張しゅっちょういたします・出張しゅっちょうなさいます） - said to a colleague.

社長しゃちょうは明日あした大阪おおさかへ**出張しゅっちょうなさいます**。

The listener is inside the company, so the president is honoured normally. 出張しゅっちょういたします would lower him. Said to a client, however, the humble version becomes correct - and he would lose his さん too.

いたす · Practice 3

3. Translate into Japanese, as an email to a client: "I'll send you the details tomorrow."

詳くわしい内容ないようは明日あしたご連絡れんらくいたします。

連絡れんらく is a Chinese-derived root, so it takes ご, and the humble いたします replaces します because the sender is the one acting. お知しらせいたします is an equally good alternative.

いたす · Practice 4

4. Find and fix the error: 恐おそれ入いりますが、こちらの用紙ようしにご記入きにゅういたしてください。

✅ 恐おそれ入いりますが、こちらの用紙ようしにご記入きにゅうください。

A request tells the listener to act, so it cannot contain a humble verb - ご記入きにゅういたしてください orders them to lower themselves. Drop the いたして and the honorific request form appears on its own.

いたす · Practice 5

5. Explain the difference in nuance: (a) 明日あした伺うかがいます。 (b) 明日あした訪問ほうもんいたします。

Both are humble, and either would be understood as "I'll visit you tomorrow". (a) uses the dedicated humble verb for visiting a person you respect. It is warmer and more personal, and it implies the visit is to you.

(b) is built on a Chinese-derived noun plus いたす, which makes it flatter and more procedural - the register of a written schedule or a formal notification. Neither is more polite than the other; (a) sounds like a person speaking, (b) like a company writing.

ございます

Meaning · the very polite form of ある - "there is", "we have"

ございます does the same work as あります, one register higher. It is 丁寧ていねい語ご: it lifts the tone of your speech, not the subject of the sentence. That is why you can use it about the listener's questions and about your own shop's stock without contradicting yourself - nobody is being raised or lowered, only the language.

ございます · Nuance & register

This is service language above all. Shops, hotels, restaurants, station announcements, formal speeches and business correspondence run on ございます; a conversation between friends does not. Saying ❌冷蔵庫れいぞうこにビールがございます to a flatmate is not politer, it is theatrical.

Because it is 丁寧ていねい語ご and not 尊敬そんけい語ご, ございます never honours anybody by itself. That has one practical consequence worth knowing: strictly, asking about something the listener possesses calls for the honorific おありです - ご質問しつもんはおありでしょうか. In real service Japanese, ご質問しつもんはございませんか is what you will hear almost every time, and it is not treated as an error. Use whichever you like; recognise both.

ございます · Nuance & register (cont.)

Three details:

ございます · Nuance & register (cont.)

  • ありがとうございます／ありがとうございました. The present form thanks for
    something still going on or just now done; the past form closes it off. A shop says ありがとうございました as you leave with the bag.
  • 申もうし訳わけございません is the standard business apology. A
    minority of usage guides object to it on the grounds that 申もうし訳わけない is a single adjective that should not be split, and prefer 申もうし訳わけなく存ぞんじます. In practice the objection has lost; you will hear ございません everywhere, and using it will not mark you.
  • The い-adjective form is dated. よろしゅうございます, うれしゅうございます
    and 大おおきゅうございます are grammatical, but they belong to older, very formal or self-consciously refined speech. The frozen greetings survive; the productive pattern has largely gone.

ございます · ございます vs あります

Rule of thumb - same meaning, different altitude. あります is polite; ございます is polite and formal, and it announces that you are in a service or ceremonial situation.

地下ちかに駐車場ちゅうしゃじょうがあります。
There's a car park in the basement.

地下ちかに駐車場ちゅうしゃじょうがございます。
There is a car park in the basement.

Nothing about the car park has changed. The second sentence tells you the speaker is behind a counter. Reach for あります in ordinary conversation, in class and with colleagues, and switch to ございます when you are serving somebody or speaking formally to a group.

ございます · ございます vs いらっしゃいます

Rule of thumb - ございます is for things and facts; いらっしゃいます is for people you are honouring.

受付うけつけに案内図あんないずがございます。
There is a floor plan at reception.

受付うけつけに係かかりの方かたがいらっしゃいます。
There is a member of staff at reception.

ございます · ございます vs いらっしゃいます (cont.)

The mistake goes one way: learners reach for ございます about a person because it feels like the most polite word available. It is not more polite about a person - it is wrong, because it treats them as an object. Note that your own side does take a non-honorific verb, but the word is おります, not ございます: 担当たんとうの者ものが受付うけつけにおります.

ございます · ございます vs でございます

Rule of thumb - ございます replaces あります; でございます replaces です. Existence versus identity.

三階さんがいに喫茶店きっさてんがございます。
There is a coffee shop on the third floor.

こちらが喫茶店きっさてんでございます。
This is the coffee shop.

ございます · ございます vs でございます (cont.)

The で is the giveaway. If the English is "there is a…" or "we have a…", you want the bare form; if it is "X is Y", you want でございます. Mixing them produces ❌こちらが喫茶店きっさてんございます, which is simply ungrammatical.

ございます · Common mistakes

  • ❌ ご用件ようけんは何なにかござりますか。 → ✅ ご用件ようけんは何なにかございますか。
    The り becomes い before ます - the same irregularity as いらっしゃいます, おっしゃいます, なさいます and くださいます.

  • ❌ お客きゃく様さまは二階にかいにございます。 → ✅ お客きゃく様さまは二階にかいにいらっしゃいます。
    A person is not a thing. ございます about a customer sounds as though they are stock on a shelf.

  • ❌ ねえ、消けしゴムございます? → ✅ ねえ、消けしゴムある?
    A register error rather than a grammar one, and the kind that makes people laugh. Service Japanese aimed at a classmate is comic.

  • ❌ ありがとうでございます。 → ✅ ありがとうございます。
    ございます follows the adjective form directly. The で belongs only after a noun.

ございます · Practice 1

1. 申もうし訳わけありません、本日ほんじつのチケットは売うり切きれて席せきが____。

申もうし訳わけありません、本日ほんじつのチケットは売うり切きれて席せきがございません。

The negative of ございます is ございません - there is no ❌ございないです. The whole sentence is service register, so ありません would sit a step lower than the rest of it.

ございます · Practice 2

2. Choose: 田中たなか様さまは会議室かいぎしつに（ございます・いらっしゃいます）。

田中たなか様さまは会議室かいぎしつにいらっしゃいます。

ございます is the polite form of ある, which is used for things. A person you are honouring takes the honorific of いる. If 田中たなか were your own colleague and you were speaking to a client, the answer would change again - to おります.

ございます · Practice 3

3. Translate into Japanese, as a hotel receptionist: "There is a convenience store next door."

隣となりにコンビニがございます。

The subject is a shop, so the verb of existence is ある, raised to service register as ございます. Note that no honorific prefix is needed on コンビニ - loanwords do not take one.

ございます · Practice 4

4. Find and fix the error: こちらが新あたらしいパンフレットございます。

✅ こちらが新あたらしいパンフレットでございます。

This sentence identifies one thing as another, so it needs the polite copula, not the polite verb of existence. Without the で there is nothing linking the noun to the sentence at all.

ございます · Practice 5

5. Explain the difference in nuance: (a) ご意見いけんはありませんか。 (b) ご意見いけんはございませんか。

Both are polite and both are correct. (a) is ordinary polite Japanese - a chair asking a familiar committee, or a teacher asking a class. (b) lifts the whole utterance into formal register, and suits a public meeting, a shareholders' briefing or a customer.

Neither honours the listener grammatically, since ございます is 丁寧ていねい語ご. If you want to raise the listener as well, the honorific of ある is おありです: ご意見いけんはおありでしょうか. That is the strictly correct form, though (b) is far more often heard.

でございます

Meaning · the formal copula - "is", at the register of a hotel desk or a station announcement

でございます replaces です. Like ございます, it is 丁寧ていねい語ご: it raises the formality of the sentence without honouring anybody in it. That is why you can say 山田やまだでございます about yourself, and why you must not say it about the person you are talking to.

でございます · Formation

Attaches to Form Example

な-adjective な-adj + でございます 結構けっこうです → 結構けっこうでございます
Negative ではございません ちがいます → ではございません
Past でございました 以上いじょうでございました
Tentative でございましょう そのとおりでございましょう
い-adjective frame not used よろしいです → よろしゅうございます

でございます · Formation (cont.)

Rule of thumb - でございます for things, places, prices, facts, and for yourself. でいらっしゃいます for the listener and for anyone else you are raising.

でございます · Examples

こちらが会議室かいぎしつでございます。
This is the meeting room.

はい、山田やまだでございます。
Yes, Yamada speaking.

お会計かいけいは三千さんぜん円えんでございます。
That comes to three thousand yen.

次つぎは終点しゅうてん、東京とうきょうでございます。
The next stop is the last one, Tokyo.

でございます · Examples (cont.)

こちらのお席せきはすべて禁煙きんえんでございます。
All of the seats in this section are non-smoking.

申もうし訳わけございませんが、その商品しょうひんは当店とうてんの取とり扱あつかいではございません。
I'm sorry, but that isn't a product we carry.

担当たんとうの者ものは本日ほんじつ休やすみでございますので、明日あしたあらためてご連絡れんらくいたします。
The person handling this is off today, so we'll be in touch again tomorrow.

本日ほんじつはお忙いそがしいところお越こしいただき、ありがとうございます。司会しかいを務つとめます佐藤さとうでございます。
Thank you for coming today despite your busy schedules. I'm Sato, and I'll be chairing.

でございます · Nuance & register

Announcements, counters, telephones and formal speeches. In a shop or a hotel it is the default copula and nobody notices it; in a conversation with a friend it would be as odd as answering the door in a uniform. Between those extremes, a business meeting will use it for set pieces - introductions, figures, conclusions - and drop back to です for ordinary discussion.

The single most useful thing to keep straight is that でございます does not honour. Saying ❌田中たなか様さまでございますか to a visitor is a real error, not merely a stiff one: it equates the guest with a price or a room number. The honorific copula is でいらっしゃいます, and it is used the same way for descriptions: お元気げんきでいらっしゃいますか, 社長しゃちょうの奥様おくさまでいらっしゃいます.

Some notes on the edges:

でございます · Nuance & register (cont.)

  • Negatives. ではございません is the formal negative. In quick speech people
    reduce it to じゃございません, which is a mixed register and is best avoided in writing.
  • い-adjectives. There is no ❌よろしいでございますか. The forms that exist are
    よろしゅうございますか, which sounds old-fashioned, and よろしいでしょうか, which is what you should actually say.
  • Placement. でございます closes the sentence. Inside a sentence you use the
    て-form でございまして, which is heavy and almost entirely a written or speech-making form.
  • Do not stack it on 敬語けいご you have already used.
    ご覧らんになるお品しなでございます is fine, but ❌ご覧らんになられるお品しなでございます piles two honorific layers under the formal copula.

でございます · でございます vs です

Rule of thumb - same meaning, different height. です is polite; でございます is polite and formal, and it signals a service or ceremonial setting.

これは去年きょねんの資料しりょうです。
This is last year's material.

こちらが昨年度さくねんどの資料しりょうでございます。
This is last year's material.

でございます · でございます vs です (cont.)

Notice how much moves with the copula. これ becomes こちら, 去年きょねん becomes 昨年度さくねんど. Register is carried by the whole sentence, and a lone でございます on top of casual vocabulary sounds like a joke.

でございます · でございます vs でいらっしゃいます

Rule of thumb - でございます about yourself and about things; でいらっしゃいます about the person you are honouring. Getting this backwards is the classic 敬語けいご error.

失礼しつれいですが、ご予約よやくの方かたでいらっしゃいますか。
Excuse me - do you have a reservation with us?

受付うけつけの木村きむらでございます。
I'm Kimura from reception.

でございます · でございます vs でいらっしゃいます (cont.)

The pair shows the whole three-way system in two lines. The first raises the guest; the second simply formalises a statement about the speaker. Swap them and you have honoured yourself while treating the guest as an object - which is why ❌私わたしが担当たんとうでいらっしゃいます is such a jarring sentence.

でございます · でございます vs ～になります

Rule of thumb - になる means "becomes". For "is", the formal copula is でございます.

こちらがお飲のみ物もののメニューでございます。
Here is the drinks menu.

合計ごうけいで二千にせん円えんでございます。
That's two thousand yen altogether.

でございます · でございます vs ～になります (cont.)

You will hear こちらメニューになります and 二千にせん円えんになります constantly in Japanese shops. It is common enough to have its own nickname - マニュアル敬語けいご, "manual keigo" - and a large number of speakers consider it wrong, on the grounds that nothing is turning into anything. になります is correct when there really is a change （四月しがつから新あたらしい料金りょうきんになります）. For a plain identification, でございます is the safe choice.

でございます · Common mistakes

  • ❌ 田中たなか様さまでございますか。 → ✅ 田中たなか様さまでいらっしゃいますか。
    でございます does not raise anybody. Asked of a guest, it puts them in the same grammatical box as the room key.

  • ❌ 私わたしは鈴木すずきでいらっしゃいます。 → ✅ 私わたしは鈴木すずきでございます。
    The mirror error, and the one learners make when they decide でいらっしゃいます is simply "the politest version".

  • ❌ お席せきはこちらでよろしいでございますか。 → ✅ お席せきはこちらでよろしいでしょうか。
    An い-adjective cannot take でございます. Either use でしょうか, or the old-fashioned よろしゅうございますか.

でございます · Common mistakes (cont.)

  • ❌ こちら、お釣つりになります。 → ✅ こちら、お釣つりでございます。
    The change is nothing to do with becoming. Very widely heard, and very widely disliked.

でございます · Practice 1

1. お電話でんわありがとうございます。山やまの上うえホテル、予約よやく係がかり____。

お電話でんわありがとうございます。山やまの上うえホテル、予約よやく係がかりでございます。

You are identifying yourself and your own department, so the formal copula is right. でいらっしゃいます here would honour yourself, which is the one thing 敬語けいご never lets you do.

でございます · Practice 2

2. Choose: 失礼しつれいですが、部長ぶちょうの奥様おくさま（でございます・でいらっしゃいます）か。

失礼しつれいですが、部長ぶちょうの奥様おくさまでいらっしゃいますか。

The sentence identifies the person in front of you, so it needs the honorific copula. でございます would be merely formal, and asking a guest ❌奥様おくさまでございますか sounds as though you are reading a label.

でございます · Practice 3

3. Translate into Japanese, as a shop assistant: "This one is this year's newest model."

こちらが今年ことしの最新さいしんモデルでございます。

A product is a thing, so the formal copula is the right choice, and こちら rather than これ keeps the whole sentence at the same height. ❌モデルになります would say the item is turning into a model.

でございます · Practice 4

4. Find and fix the error: 田中たなか様さま、こちらがご注文ちゅうもんの品しなになります。合計ごうけいで千五百せんごひゃく円えんになります。

✅ 田中たなか様さま、こちらがご注文ちゅうもんの品しなでございます。合計ごうけいで千五百せんごひゃく円えんでございます。

Neither the goods nor the total is becoming anything, so になります has no work to do. Both clauses are plain identifications and take the formal copula.

でございます · Practice 5

5. Explain the difference in nuance: (a) 受付うけつけは一階いっかいです。 (b) 受付うけつけは一階いっかいでございます。

Both are correct and neither honours anyone - でございます is 丁寧ていねい語ご, so the difference is formality, not respect.

(a) is ordinary polite Japanese: a colleague telling a visiting friend where to go, or a sign in a small office. (b) places the speaker on the serving side of a counter. Said by a hotel employee it is exactly right; said by one student to another it sounds like a performance.
